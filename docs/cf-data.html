Data

Use cf-data with exactly one of src (HTTP(S) JSON) or source (a global own-property path), plus a valid identifier name and a direct template. Global values, promises and functions are supported; functions are called without a receiver. Loading and error templates use data-state. JSON requests share a page-lifetime cache, time out after ten seconds, and failures may be retried by a new element. Rendering is one-time and atomic; nested data providers resolve independently from globals with their own aliases.
